About

FaceFlower covers faces and license plates in a photo before you share it. Faces get flower icons. Plates get a NO NUMBER sticker. Detection and drawing stay in the browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Projects

This page lets you add a photo, find faces and plates, paint covers on a copy, and download the result. pico.js finds faces. FaceFlower code in this repository finds plates and draws the covers.

Help

Press Upload or drop a JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo onto the original pane, then press Convert. Use Cover size − / + if the flowers or stickers should be smaller or larger. Right-click (or click) a cover and choose Remove flower or Remove plate if that person or plate should stay visible. For a plate sticker that missed the number, choose Move plate (or drag the sticker) and drop it on the plate. Press Download for a PNG named faceflower.png.

Faces are covered with pico.js (MIT). Plates use FaceFlower code in this repo (MIT). Nothing is sent to a server.
